# Store listing draft

**App name (max 30):** Pagewise Reader

**Short description (max 80):**
Read EPUB, PDF and text without pinching or zooming. Shelves, highlights, flip pages.

**Full description (max 4000):**
Reading on a phone should not feel like fighting the page. Pagewise Reader turns your books into text that fits your screen, so there is no pinching, zooming, or sideways scrolling.

READ COMFORTABLY
- Open EPUB, PDF (text-based) and plain text files
- Choose your font, text size, line spacing and side margins
- Themes: Light, Sepia, Dark, Sage and pure-black Night
- Brightness dimmer below your phone's minimum, plus a warm light filter for night reading

FEELS LIKE A BOOK
- A page-flip animation, or slide, or none
- One page, two-page spread, or scroll
- Tap the screen edges or swipe to turn pages; drag the bar to jump anywhere

MAKE IT YOURS
- A bookshelf with covers, grouped into shelves you name
- Progress on every book, plus finished count and reading time
- Bookmarks, four highlight colors, and notes
- Touch lock so a stray tap never turns the page
- Accent colors and shelf styles

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
Your books and notes stay on your device. An account is optional, with no ads and no tracking.

Note: scanned PDFs (photos of pages) have no text to reflow and are not supported yet.
