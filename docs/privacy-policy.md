# Privacy Policy for Pagewise Reader

Last updated: [DATE]

Pagewise Reader ("the app") is a reading app by [YOUR NAME OR COMPANY].

## Summary
Without an account, everything stays on your device. Accounts created without a server connection are also kept only on your device. If you create an account, we store your email address and your reading data (progress, bookmarks, highlights, shelves, settings) so they sync across your devices. We never sell or share it.

## What the app stores on your device
- The book files you choose to open (EPUB, PDF, text), kept inside the app's private storage
- Your reading progress, bookmarks, highlights, notes, shelves, reading time, and display settings

## If you create an account
- Your email address and password (stored hashed by our authentication provider, Supabase) are used only to log you in
- Your reading data listed above is stored on our Supabase project and is only readable by your own account
- Book files and your AI key are never uploaded
- You can log out at any time; to delete your account and data, contact us at the address below

## What we do not do
- No analytics, advertising, or tracking
- Nothing is sent anywhere unless you create an account or use AI (when you use AI, the chapter you are reading is sent to Anthropic)

## Your control
You can remove any book in the app. Uninstalling the app or clearing its storage in Android settings deletes all data it holds.

## Children
The app is not directed at children under 13 and collects no data from anyone.

## Changes
If this policy changes, the Play Store data safety form will be updated to match.
